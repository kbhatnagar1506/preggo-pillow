How your data is protected

  • You’re signed out after 15 minutes without activity, and after 12 hours no matter what.
  • The session cookie is signed, and the server stores only a SHA‑256 hash of the token — a stolen database row cannot be replayed as a login.
  • Identity comes from Auth0 over OpenID Connect with PKCE, a single‑use transaction cookie, and full ID‑token validation pinned to RS256.

Where your data goes

  • Nothing leaves this device.

Preggo Pillow is not a medical device and does not diagnose. It counts movement and tells you when the pattern changes against your own baseline, which is the one thing a guideline says is worth acting on.
